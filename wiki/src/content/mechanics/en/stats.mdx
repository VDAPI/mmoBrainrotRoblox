## Attributes

A character has four attributes: **Strength**, **Dexterity**, **Intellect** and **Vitality**. Every class starts with a different set; the class's main attributes are highlighted.

<ClassStartTable />

## Points per level

Every level up gives you <Stat path="progression.statPointsPerLevel" /> attribute points to spend in the Character window (<Key action="win_character" />). Skill ranks grow with your character level by themselves (maximum by level <Stat path="skills.rules.ranks.capLevel" /> at the latest); the Skills window (<Key action="win_skills" />) shows the level of the next rank. Class path points: coming soon.

## Formulas

Attributes turn into stats by the game's formulas. Bonuses from gear, blessings and the guild are added on top. Dexterity gives its full crit chance, attack speed and dodge up to <Stat path="mechanics.dexRatingLevel" format="lvl" />; after that each point gives proportionally less, while attack from Dexterity stays the same.

## Resetting points

You take back spent attribute points with a <Item id="scroll_oblivion" />: the teacher in the city sells it and the price grows with your level. The <Item id="scroll_skillreset" /> does nothing for now: it returns with class paths (coming soon).
