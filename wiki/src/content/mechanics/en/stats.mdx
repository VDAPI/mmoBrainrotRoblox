## Attributes

A character has four attributes: **Strength**, **Dexterity**, **Intellect** and **Vitality**. Every class starts with a different set; the class's main attributes are highlighted.

## Points per level

Every level up gives you <Stat path="progression.statPointsPerLevel" /> attribute points to spend in the Backpack window (<Key action="win_inventory" />). You buy skill ranks with skill points: every weapon pool has <Stat path="skills.rules.skillPointsByLevel.0" /> of them at the first level and one more with every level up, and the rank cap grows with the level; the Skills window (<Key action="win_skills" />) shows from which level the next rank can be bought. Every <Stat path="skills.rules.pathPointsEvery" /> levels you get a talent point to spend in the same window on direction nodes (stats, skill changes, party auras).

## Formulas

Attributes turn into stats by the game's formulas. Bonuses from gear, blessings and the guild are added on top. Dexterity gives its full crit chance, attack speed and dodge up to <Stat path="mechanics.dexRatingLevel" format="lvl" />; after that each point gives proportionally less, while attack from Dexterity stays the same.

Energy regenerates more slowly in combat than outside it (<Stat path="stats.formula.energyRegenBase" /> a second outside combat, <Stat path="stats.formula.energyRegenCombatMul" format="x" /> of that in combat), so in a longer fight Warriors and Hunters drink energy potions. Energy regen bonuses from gear and paths work in full in combat.

## Legendary and Mythic items

Legendary and Mythic items are only a little stronger in stats than Heroic ones (base multiplier <Stat path="rarities.rarities[legendary].baseMul" format="x" /> and <Stat path="rarities.rarities[mythic].baseMul" format="x" /> against <Stat path="rarities.rarities[heroic].baseMul" format="x" />); what sets them apart are effects that change the skills of the class. A Legendary item has <Stat path="rarities.rarities[legendary].legendaryEffects" /> effect from the class's legendary pool, a Mythic one <Stat path="rarities.rarities[mythic].legendaryEffects" /> legendary and <Stat path="rarities.rarities[mythic].mythicEffects" /> mythic, for example a Meteor that breaks into smaller blasts or a Charge that stuns longer. An effect works only for its class, together with breakpoints and path nodes, and the same effect from two items works once. The effects an item can roll are on its page.

## Limits

Some stats have an upper limit. Anything above the limit does nothing, so once you reach it, look for other bonuses.

## Resetting points

You take back spent attribute points with a <Item id="scroll_oblivion" />: the teacher in the city sells it and the price grows with your level. The <Item id="scroll_skillreset" /> resets the ranks of every weapon pool and the direction nodes at once; the teacher sells it and so does the premium shop.
