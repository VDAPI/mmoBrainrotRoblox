## Attributes

A character has four attributes: **Strength**, **Dexterity**, **Intellect** and **Vitality**. Every class starts with a different set; the class's main attributes are highlighted.

## Points per level

Every level up gives you <Stat path="progression.statPointsPerLevel" /> attribute points to spend in the Character window (<Key action="win_character" />). From level <Stat path="progression.skillPointsFromLevel" /> you also get <Stat path="progression.skillPointsPerLevel" /> skill point per level; spend it in the Skills window (<Key action="win_skills" />) or plan it in the <Link to="classes">class planner</Link>.

Attributes turn into stats by the game's formulas. Bonuses from gear, blessings and the guild are added on top.

## Limits

Some stats have an upper limit. Anything above the limit does nothing, so once you reach it, look for other bonuses.

## Resetting points

You take back spent attribute points with a <Item id="scroll_oblivion" />: the teacher in the city sells it and the price grows with your level. Skill points are reset by a separate <Item id="scroll_skillreset" />.
