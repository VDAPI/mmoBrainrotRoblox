<Callout kind="tip" title="No power">
Cosmetics, pets and passes give no stats at all. A character in an outfit fights exactly like one without it. The game's wardrobe says: “Click an item to try it on. Looks only — no stats.”
</Callout>

## Wardrobe

Cosmetics belong to the account, so every one of your characters can use them. You put them on in the wardrobe in the game menu. There is one slot per kind: an outfit, an aura, a trail, a weapon effect and a pet at the same time.

## Passes

Passes give comfort, not power. They also add room in every backpack, further character slots and saved looks in the wardrobe.

## Dragon Currency services

Dragon Currency is an account currency. In the premium shop it buys cosmetics and a few services. The Scroll of Protection has a daily limit, so nobody can buy an edge in upgrading.

<PassList shop />

The Elixir of Wholeness restores all health, mana and energy at once. It has its own cooldown and a daily limit, is bound to the account and does not work in a duel, on the arena, in boss dungeons or right after a fight with another player. The Cloak of Luring is time that runs while you are offline too: every few seconds it draws ordinary monsters from nearby onto you, never elites or bosses, and it works only in the open world, outside the city, caves and red zones. It grants no strength and no loot, it only speeds up farming.

You get Dragon Currency in the game shop for Robux and in rare events. The wiki lists no prices: always check them in the game.
