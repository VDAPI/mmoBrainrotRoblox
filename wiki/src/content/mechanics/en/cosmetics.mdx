<Callout kind="tip" title="No power">
Cosmetics, pets and passes give no stats at all. A character in an outfit fights exactly like one without it. The game's wardrobe says: “Click an item to try it on. Looks only — no stats.”
</Callout>

## Wardrobe

Cosmetics belong to the account, so every one of your characters can use them. You put them on in the wardrobe in the game menu. There is one slot per kind: an outfit, an aura, a trail, a weapon effect and a pet at the same time.

The wardrobe also holds marks other players see: a frame around your portrait (on the character bar, in the character window and in your profile), a frame for your name in chat together with your speech bubble's colours, titles over your character (picked in the same place as the titles earned in the game), gestures from the emote wheel or the `/e` command (everyone starts with two, none works in combat or on a mount), an effect shown when you level up and dyes that recolour the outfit you wear.

## Character appearance

Every class has its own garb: the Warrior wears plate and a cape, the Cleric a robe with a hood and a holy symbol, the Hunter leathers, a hood and a quiver on the back, and the Mage a floor-length robe with a high collar and a book at the belt. Every character has the same standard proportions, so fighting and riding look the same on everyone.

In the character creator you pick only the face (<Stat path="classlooks.faces" /> to choose from), the skin colour (<Stat path="classlooks.skins.length" /> tones) and the hairstyle (<Stat path="classlooks.hairs" /> styles). The class gives the rest. Every character has one free change of appearance, which you make from the wardrobe anywhere. Any later change costs gold at <Npc id="barber" /> on the market square.

In the wardrobe every class has, besides its class garb, outfits for Dragon Currency and two outfits you can only earn in the game: one for finishing the second chapter of the story, one for defeating a boss with that class. A class outfit replaces the class's default clothing. You can buy an outfit of another class, but only a character of that class can wear it. Shared outfits fit every class, and dyes recolour the class garb too.

The settings have the option "Show my Roblox avatar". Your character then looks like your avatar, but in standard proportions, and the wardrobe outfit goes over it. Other players see what you chose.

<Callout kind="tip" title="Looks only">
Outfits, faces, skin, hair and the avatar give no stats at all. Every player has the same proportions, also in fights against other players.
</Callout>
