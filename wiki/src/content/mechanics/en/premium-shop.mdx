<Callout kind="tip" title="No advantage for money">
Real money buys looks and comfort, never character power. Rankings, the arena, the Blood Moon and the Champion's Tournament do not depend on purchases.
</Callout>

## Dragon Shards

Dragon Shards are the account's currency: every character of yours pays from the same balance. You buy them in the game's shop for Robux, and a few events and ranking rewards pay them for playing. The balance is always shown at the top of the shop, next to the "Top up" button. The wiki shows no prices and no amounts of shards because they change with the offer: always check them in the game.

You open the shop from the on-screen menu. It has tabs: currency, passes, items, cosmetics and mounts (the last one only when something is on sale). On a computer the tabs stand in a column on the left, on a phone in a scrolling strip under the header. Every purchase for shards first shows a window with the name, what it gives, the price and the balance after it.

## Passes

A pass is bought once and stays on the account for good. Passes give space and comfort: loot pickup around you, a bigger deposit, a faster return after death, extra slots in every backpack, more character slots and saved looks in the wardrobe. ## Items and services

Scrolls redistribute points, unbind an item or keep an upgrade from dropping. The Scroll of Protection has a daily limit, so nobody can buy an edge in upgrading. The services are a new name for the character and Cloak of Luring time, and the premium backpacks give room only, with no level requirement and no stats.

## Elixir of Wholeness, Cloak of Luring, Auto Potions and Auto Hunt

These are the two explicit exceptions to "looks and comfort only". The Elixir of Wholeness restores all health, mana and energy at once, but never in fights with players or at bosses. The Cloak of Luring speeds up farming ordinary monsters, gives no power and no better loot, and its time runs while you are offline too.

Auto Potions are a comfort: your character drinks the potions of its bar by itself at the health and mana / energy thresholds you set, with the same cooldown as the key and never in fights with players. Auto Hunt is the third exception: your character walks over the whole hunting ground and fights its ordinary monsters by itself, for a few hours a day at most, with less experience and gold and outside the weekly ranking. Its time can also be earned for free: the Scroll of the Hunt drops from bosses.

## Offer of the week and starter packs

Every week, from Monday midnight UTC, the cosmetics tab shows four chosen cosmetics, one of them cheaper. The offer is the same on every server and changes by itself.

Starter packs can be bought once per account. They give Dragon Shards, cosmetics found only in packs, and one of them also a pass with extra backpack slots. After the purchase they leave the shop.

## What is never sold

The shop never sells experience, gold, items with bonuses, upgrade materials, blessings, experience or loot potions, or extra entries to boss dungeons. The VIP gives no experience and no stats. Rankings, the arena rating and event points count only what you do in the game, so an account with purchases and one without stand on equal terms. An automatic test checks every new thing in the shop and rejects anything that would give power.
