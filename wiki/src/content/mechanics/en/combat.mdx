## Damage step by step

The server counts everything, in this order:

1. **Hit.** The target's dodge chance minus your accuracy, at most <Stat path="mechanics.combat.dodgeCap" format="pct" />. A dodge means no damage. Monsters dodge only your basic attacks (skills always hit them); you can dodge a monster's regular attack (not a red zone) and another player's hits.
2. **Strength of the blow.** Your attack (physical or magic) times the skill multiplier.
3. **Armour.** The target's defence (after your armour penetration) stops part of the damage, a smaller part the higher your level. Magic uses magic resistance the same way.
4. **Randomness.** The result is multiplied by a value from <Stat path="mechanics.combat.randomMin" format="frac" /> to <Stat path="mechanics.combat.randomMax" format="frac" />.
5. **Critical hit.** The base chance is <Stat path="mechanics.formula.critBase" format="pct" />, at most <Stat path="stats.stats[critChance].cap" format="pct" />. A crit deals <Stat path="mechanics.formula.critDmgBase" format="pct" /> damage.
6. **Element.** The target's resistance to the attack's element reduces the damage, by at most <Stat path="stats.stats[resFire].cap" format="pct" />.
7. **PvP.** Against a player the result is multiplied by <Stat path="mechanics.combat.pvpModifier" format="frac" />.

Every hit deals at least <Stat path="mechanics.combat.minDamage" /> point of damage.

## Monster ranks

Elites are bigger, stronger and higher level than normal monsters of their kind. Elite II fight like a small boss and need a party.

An Elite II announces a **Crushing Blow**: a dark crimson circle under its target that you must leave before it lands. At its own level it takes a big part of your health, and the higher it is above you, the more dangerous it gets: a few levels above you it kills in one hit. Running away does not save you: the Elite II throws at a target that keeps it at a distance (the harder, the lower the target is below it), pounces on it, and announces the Crushing Blow from afar too. An ordinary Elite has a **Heavy Blow**, a cone in front of it to dodge.

Every Elite II has its own 2–3 kinds of items (for example only a helmet, gloves and an off-hand): its bestiary card and the cave page say what it drops. In every level band the Elite IIs together drop the whole equipment, weapons and jewellery too. Every cave has two of them, in distant rooms, each with its own respawn and different loot.

The region maps also have **lairs** marked with a skull: a small corner with an Elite II in the middle, its guards around it and gathering nodes at the edge. A lair's Elite II has its own loot like the cave ones, but it comes back less often and quietly: the map only shows the place, never whether the Elite II is alive. Lairs keep the zone of their map, and Auto Hunt, the Cloak of Luring and world events leave them alone. Every region page lists its lairs.
