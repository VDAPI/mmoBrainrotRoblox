## Damage step by step

The server counts everything, in this order:

1. **Hit.** The target's dodge chance minus your accuracy, at most <Stat path="mechanics.combat.dodgeCap" format="pct" />. A dodge means no damage.
2. **Strength of the blow.** Your attack (physical or magic) times the skill multiplier.
3. **Armour.** The target's defence (after your armour penetration) stops part of the damage, a smaller part the higher your level. Magic uses magic resistance the same way.
4. **Randomness.** The result is multiplied by a value from <Stat path="mechanics.combat.randomMin" format="frac" /> to <Stat path="mechanics.combat.randomMax" format="frac" />.
5. **Critical hit.** The base chance is <Stat path="mechanics.formula.critBase" format="pct" />, at most <Stat path="stats.stats[critChance].cap" format="pct" />. A crit deals <Stat path="mechanics.formula.critDmgBase" format="pct" /> damage.
6. **Element.** The target's resistance to the attack's element reduces the damage, by at most <Stat path="stats.stats[resFire].cap" format="pct" />.
7. **PvP.** Against a player the result is multiplied by <Stat path="mechanics.combat.pvpModifier" format="frac" />.

Every hit deals at least <Stat path="mechanics.combat.minDamage" /> point of damage.

## Monster ranks

Elites are bigger, stronger and higher level than normal monsters of their kind. Elite II fight like a small boss and need a party.

<VariantTable />

Monsters in a group hit weaker one by one but attack together. Monsters far below your level (<Stat path="mechanics.combat.passiveLevelGap" /> levels or more) do not attack first.
