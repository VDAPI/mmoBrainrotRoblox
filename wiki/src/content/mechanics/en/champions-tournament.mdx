<Callout kind="tip" title="Seeded by rating">
Your place in the bracket depends only on your arena rating and the time of your signup. Purchases change nothing here.
</Callout>

## When and signups

The tournament takes place once a week by the UTC clock. You sign up with <Npc id="arenamaster" /> or on the "Tournament" tab of the arena window, from any server, in the signup window before the start. Your range is the arena range of your level at the moment of the signup.

<EventSchedule event="tourney" />

When a range has more players than places, the highest ratings get in, and on an equal rating whoever signed up earlier. Before the start the signed players get an invitation: once accepted, the game moves them to the tournament server with a lobby and several fighting fields.

## The bracket

The tournament is a single elimination one on one bracket: the loser is out. Seeding follows the rating, and when the number of players is not a power of two, the best seeds get a bye to the next round. A player who does not show up for a match loses by walkover. There is no match for third place.

Too few signed or present players in a range cancel the tournament in that range, with a message for those who signed up.

## Match rules

A match is fought like in the arena: to two won rounds, full health and mana at the start of each round, a few potions per match. Tournament matches do not change the rating. The Elixir of Wholeness and the Cloak of Luring do not work in the tournament. Players who lost can stay and watch or return to their server.

## Rewards and the statue

Rewards come by mail. The champion of a range gets a title for a few days, an aura for good and Dragon Shards; the finalist a frame and Dragon Shards; the semifinalists Dragon Shards; and every participant Arena Tokens. The champions of the last weeks stand on the Tournament Hall of Fame board in the city, and the victory is announced to the whole world.
