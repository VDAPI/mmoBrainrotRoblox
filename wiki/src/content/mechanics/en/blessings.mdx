## How they work

A blessing is an item that gives a stat bonus for <Stat path="blessings.duration" format="min" /> once used. Only one works at a time: a new one replaces the old one. The time runs even while you are offline, and death does not stop it.

There are five lines, each in five rarities. A higher rarity gives more and adds new stats (highlighted in gold). The rarest blessing of every line also adds an EXP and loot bonus.

<BlessingTable />

<Callout kind="tip" title="Which line to pick">
Warriors and Hunters take their own lines, Mages and Clerics the Sage line, tanks the Guardian line. For levelling and farming gold, Fortune is the best.
</Callout>

You get blessings from daily contracts and some main quests. The alchemist also brews every rarity except the highest (see <Link to="crafting" hash="alchemy">Crafting</Link>).

## Resistance elixirs

An elixir raises one elemental resistance. It works on its own, next to a blessing, so you can have both at once.

## Potions
