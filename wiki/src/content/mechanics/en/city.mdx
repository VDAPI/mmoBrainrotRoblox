The city is a green zone: nobody can attack you here. You start the game here, respawn here and come back for shopping, upgrades and quests. On the world map it is <Region id="city" />.

## Townsfolk and services

Every townsperson stands by their building or stall. Press <Key action="interact" /> next to them to talk or open their service.

<CityNpcs />

## Arena

East of the market stands the arena. Only there can you challenge another player to a duel, even though the rest of the city is safe. Duel rules are in <Link to="mechanics" id="pvp" hash="arena-and-duels">PvP</Link>.

## Gates to the regions

You leave the city through its gates, each leading to a different region. You pick the target area and the game suggests the best one for your level. The <Link to="map">world map</Link> shows every route.
