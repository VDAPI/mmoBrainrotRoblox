## Zones

Every map has a zone colour. You see it on the banner when you enter a map and next to the map name on the wiki. The zone that counts is the one the **target** stands in.

<ZoneCards />

## The PvP toggle

The toggle is the icon in the bottom right corner of the screen. It only matters in yellow zones: nobody fights in green zones, everybody fights in red ones.

- Switching takes <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpToggleDelay" format="s" />. A countdown shows above the icon.
- For <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpCombatLock" format="s" /> after you deal or take damage from a player you cannot turn PvP off.
- The dot next to a name tells you the state: green is off, red is on or forced by the zone, pulsing is switching, grey with a padlock is locked.

## Who can attack whom

Your party and your guild are always protected, whatever the zone. A guild leader can turn the guild protection off.

<PvpMatrix />

## Fighting a player

- Damage between players is multiplied by <Stat path="mechanics.combat.pvpModifier" format="frac" />, so fights last longer than against monsters.
- Stuns, slows and other control effects last <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpCcMultiplier" format="frac" /> of their normal time on players.
- A kill counts for the player who last hit the victim within <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpKillCredit" format="s" />.
- After you respawn, nobody can attack you for <Stat path="mechanics.config.RespawnShieldSeconds" format="s" />. Attacking ends that protection at once.

<Callout kind="tip" title="When someone attacks you">
In a yellow zone, turn PvP off and walk away: the toggle works once the lock ends. In a red zone, run for the cave exit or fight back with your party.
</Callout>

## Arena and duels

On the city arena you can challenge a player to a duel, even in the green zone. An invitation lasts <Stat path="mechanics.config.DuelInviteSeconds" format="s" />, the fight starts after a <Stat path="mechanics.config.DuelCountdown" format="s" /> countdown and lasts at most <Stat path="mechanics.config.DuelTimeLimit" format="min" />. During a duel you only fight each other and nobody loses items.

## Titles

Titles come from your level, the main story, duels and fights with players. The title you pick shows above your head.
