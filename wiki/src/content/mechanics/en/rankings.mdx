<Callout kind="tip" title="Fair for everyone">
Rankings count only the game: level, experience, the arena rating and event points. Purchases, the VIP and Dragon Shards change nothing in them.
</Callout>

## Where to see them

You open the "Rankings" window from the on-screen menu or with <Key action="win_rankings" />. It has a tab per board, your own line with your place at the top and the time of the last refresh at the bottom. The top ten of the level ranking and the Hall of Fame are also on the board by the town hall, next to marble statues of the best in their class poses, with gilded trims and lights at night. Some boards are also on the <Link to="rankings">rankings page of the wiki</Link>.

Rankings are shared by all servers. When the Roblox services do not answer, the window says plainly that it shows this server only instead of an empty list. Boards refresh every few minutes, so a new level may show up with a delay.

## Level and classes

The level ranking covers every character, and separate boards cover each class. A higher level stands higher, and on the same level a fuller experience bar. On the highest level, whoever got there first stands higher. Staff characters and brand-new characters are left out, and deleting a character removes its entry.

## Hall of Fame

The first characters of each class to reach the highest level enter the Hall of Fame for good. The entry never goes away and cannot be lost. Free places show as empty rows in the window.

## Time played

The "Time played" board counts the whole minutes played on a character. Like the other boards it has a line with your place, and the rankings window shows a podium of the top three above the table.

## Weekly experience

The weekly board counts experience earned since Monday midnight UTC: from monsters, bosses, quests, contracts and world events. Players compete in level ranges, and the range follows the character's level. After the week ends, the three best characters of each range get Dragon Shards by mail and a title for a week. One server pays, once a week.

## Guilds

The guild ranking adds up the levels of the highest members. On an equal score the older guild stands higher.

## Arena

The arena tabs show the season rating in each level range, separately for one on one and three on three. The rating rules are on the <Link to="mechanics" id="arena">Arena</Link> page.
