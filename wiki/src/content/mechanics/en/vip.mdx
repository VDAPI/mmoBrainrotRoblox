<Callout kind="tip" title="Comfort, not power">
The VIP shortens the way and adds looks, but gives no experience, gold, better loot or stats. A character with VIP fights exactly like one without it.
</Callout>

## The subscription

The VIP is a monthly Roblox subscription bought in the game's shop, on the passes tab, or in the VIP window of the game menu. It covers the whole account, so every character of yours. While it is active, the benefits work on every server. The VIP window shows the status, the renewal date, the benefits, the free return with its countdown and the cosmetic of the current month.

## Benefits

<VipBenefits />

The free return works like a Stone of Return: the same channel and the same combat blocks, only no stone is used. The free respawn appears on the death screen. The daily Dragon Shards arrive on the first visit with an active VIP on a UTC day, once per account, even when you play on several servers.

## Cosmetic of the month

Every month has its own cosmetic. With an active VIP in that month you get it on your first visit in the month, and it stays on the account for good, also after the subscription ends. These cosmetics cannot be bought in the shop.

## What the VIP does not give

The VIP gives no experience, gold, loot, materials, blessings or stats. It does not change rankings, the arena rating or event points. The free return does not work on arena and tournament servers, and during the Blood Moon the event maps have no instant respawn, free or paid.

## Managing the subscription

You renew or cancel the subscription in your Roblox account settings, in the subscriptions part. After a cancellation the benefits last until the end of the paid period. The VIP title leaves the title list when the subscription ends; the cosmetics of the month stay.
