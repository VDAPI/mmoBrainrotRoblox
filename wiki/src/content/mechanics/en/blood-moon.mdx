<Callout kind="warn" title="The whole region is a red zone">
During the Blood Moon anyone can attack anyone in the open regions and the PvP switch does nothing. Death takes no items, but the event maps have no instant respawn, neither paid nor the free VIP one. The city stays safe.
</Callout>

## When

The Blood Moon rises at fixed times by the UTC clock, the same on every server. A few minutes before the start and at the start the whole server sees an announcement, and a counter on the screen shows the time to the start and to the end.

<EventSchedule event="bloodMoon" />

## What happens

The four open regions become a red zone (their caves always are one) and the sky above them turns red. Points count in the regions and in their caves. An ordinary monster group respawning in an open region may come back as an Elite. The city, boss dungeons and arena servers take no part. World events do not start during it.

The Elixir of Wholeness and the Cloak of Luring do not work meanwhile.

## Points

You earn points for defeated players and for Elites. A player far below your level gives nothing, and the same player counts only a few times per event. Dying takes no points away. An Elite's points go to whoever dealt the most damage, together with their party nearby.

## Region ranking

Every region has its own ranking, counted where you earned the points. You open the results window from the menu during the event: it shows the region's top ten and your own line. Without the shared Roblox services the ranking covers your server only, and the window says so. On equal points, whoever scored first stands higher.

## Rewards

Rewards come by mail after the event, once per event. Taking part pays Arena Tokens and a blessing; the best of a region get a title for a few days, a portrait frame and Dragon Shards, and the winner of a region is announced to the whole world.
