## Founding

You found a guild with <Npc id="guildmaster" />. You need level <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.createLevel" /> and <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.createCost" format="gold" />.

- Name: <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.nameMin" /> to <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.nameMax" /> characters, unique.
- Tag: <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.tagMin" /> to <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.tagMax" /> capital letters or digits. It shows above every member's head.
- A guild belongs to a character, not to the account: each of your characters can be in a different one.

## Treasury and skills

Every member can deposit gold into the treasury. With treasury gold the leader and officers buy guild skills, each up to level <Stat path="mechanics.guild.maxLevel" />. The bonuses work for every member, including those who join later.

A new guild has <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.baseMembers" /> places. Expansion raises the limit to <Stat path="mechanics.guild.memberLimits.5" />.

## Chat and protection

- Guild chat works across servers, and the member list shows who is playing and where.
- Guild members cannot attack each other in any zone. The leader can turn that protection off in the guild settings (see <Link to="mechanics" id="pvp">PvP</Link>).
