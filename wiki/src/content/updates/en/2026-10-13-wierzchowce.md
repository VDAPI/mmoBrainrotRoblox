title: "Mounts"
summary: "A mount of your own from level twenty, upgrades at the stablemaster, boss mounts and their looks"
---
From level twenty the stablemaster in the city gives you your first horse. You upgrade your mount with him for gold and monster parts, and each region boss can leave you a rare mount of its own. You choose the mount look in the wardrobe and it works on every mount you own. Mounts give no edge in combat: they all share the same top speed.
