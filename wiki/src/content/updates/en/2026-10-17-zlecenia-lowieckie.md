title: "Hunting contracts and the Huntmaster"
summary: "A hunting contract names one kind of monster, many contracts at once handed in with one button, Hunter's Marks and the Huntmaster's shop"
---
A hunting contract now names one kind of monster on its hunting ground, and only that kind counts. You may hold every contract up to your level at once: one kill counts for each contract it fits, and at the quest board, with the new Huntmaster Bartosz in the city or with the land's scout you take and hand them in with one button. Every contract you hand in pays Hunter's Marks, which the Huntmaster trades for potions, stones, blessings, essence with a weekly limit, a Hunter's Chest with an item for your class and cosmetics. Characters who handed contracts in before got the Marks for each of them.
