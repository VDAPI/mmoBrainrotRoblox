The quests page now shows the story in chapters, from the Whispering Meadows to the finale on Frostpeak, with the new quests of every chapter. New are the hunting contracts for every level from 1 to 99, the side quests of the people of Duskwood, the Ashen Wastes and Frostpeak, the scouts' boss quests and the weekly contracts from the board in the city. You still reveal a quest's story only on its page, and the cutscenes stay for you to see in the game.
