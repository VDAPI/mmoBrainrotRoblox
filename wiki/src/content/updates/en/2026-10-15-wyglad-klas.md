title: "Class looks"
summary: "Every class has its own garb, the creator has face, skin and hair, and the wardrobe has class outfits"
---
Characters now dress by class: the Warrior in plate, the Cleric in a hooded robe, the Hunter in leathers with a quiver and the Mage in a robe with a high collar. In the creator you pick the face, skin colour and hairstyle, and one change of appearance is free. The barber on the market square does the later ones. Class outfits wait in the wardrobe, two of them earned in the game, and the settings can show your Roblox avatar in standard proportions. The Cosmetics page explains it all under "Character appearance".
