Hrimvald, Forefather of Giants, wakes every morning and every evening at the same Polish times and stays until slain. Everyone with enough contribution gets a reward, a bigger contribution raises the chance of a Legendary and a Mythic, and every class has two Mythic items of Hrimvald that are certain after a run of fights without one. Whoever left before the end gets the reward by mail. On the wiki the boss has its own page with times, rewards and every item.

Dungeon bosses and the world boss now throw projectiles, waves, rings and cones you escape by moving; every pattern is described in the phases on the boss pages. Elites, Elite IIs and dungeon bosses have a level lock, and guilds got levels, weekly goals with the Rally, an item vault, the veteran rank and a banner. New articles cover daily and weekly contracts as sets with a streak and a bonus, the bestiary in the achievements window (with the land expert titles), the wandering merchant with times and goods, and the map's symbols and filters together with phone settings.
