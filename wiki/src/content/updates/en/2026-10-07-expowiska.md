title: Hunting grounds with character
summary: Fast EXP, better drops or more gold at the same level
---
Monster areas have profiles: ⚡ fast EXP (bigger groups, quick respawn), ✦ better drops (tougher monsters, good items
more often) and ⛁ more gold. The icon shows at the portal, on the map and on the area banner.
