<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- In the city nobody can attack you, in regions only if you both agree, in caves anyone can attack anyone.
- Your party and guild always protect you from each other.
- When someone chases you in a red zone, run for the exit or to your party.
</Callout>

## Three colours

<ZoneCards compact />

You see the zone colour on the banner when you enter a map. The zone that counts is the one the target stands in.

## The toggle

In a yellow zone you decide whether you fight players. Switching takes <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpToggleDelay" format="s" />, and after a fight with a player you cannot turn PvP off for <Stat path="mechanics.config.PvpCombatLock" format="s" />. Only turn it on when you really want to fight.

## Caves

Caves are a red zone: the toggle does nothing and anyone can attack you. In return they give the best EXP and loot for your level. Go in with a party, keep spare potions and watch other players.

## When someone attacks you

- Damage between players is lower than from monsters, so you have a moment to decide.
- In a yellow zone turn PvP off and walk away; after the lock the toggle works.
- In a red zone run for the exit or fight back with your party.
- After you die, nobody can attack you for <Stat path="mechanics.config.RespawnShieldSeconds" format="s" />.

## Party and guild protection

You cannot attack members of your party or guild in any zone, and they cannot attack you. The guild leader can turn guild protection off.

## Duels and titles

If you want to test yourself against someone without risk, challenge them to a duel on the city arena. Duels won and players defeated unlock titles.
