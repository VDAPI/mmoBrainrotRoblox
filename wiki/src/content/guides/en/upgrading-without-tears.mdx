<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- A failure never destroys an item: it moves it back one upgrade level.
- Do the low levels without protection and the high ones with a scroll.
- Upgrade an item you will keep for a while, not one you are about to replace.
</Callout>

## How it works

You upgrade at the blacksmith. Every attempt costs gold and essences, and the chance drops with every level: from a certain +1 to <Stat path="upgrade.steps.8.chance" format="frac" /> for the last step. Every upgrade level raises the item's base stats.

<UpgradeChances />

## Failure

A failed attempt moves the item back one level: +6 becomes +5. The item does not disappear and keeps its bonuses. A <Item id="scroll_protection" /> guards against that drop for one attempt: on a failure the item stays at its level and the scroll is used up.

## Materials

Low levels cost <Item id="essence_dust" />, middle ones <Item id="essence_shard" /> and the highest <Item id="essence_crystal" />. Legendary and mythic items also need a <Item id="legend_core" /> on the highest levels. You get essences from dismantling items, from quests and contracts (see <Link to="crafting" hash="dismantle">Crafting</Link>).

## A simple plan

- On low levels the chance is high and a drop costs little: upgrade without a scroll, roughly up to +4.
- Higher up every failure takes away an expensive step. Compare the average attempts without and with a scroll in the table: from that point on the scroll saves gold and essences.
- Do the last levels on an item you will keep for a long time.
