<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- You can found a guild from level <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.createLevel" /> for <Stat path="mechanics.guild.rules.createCost" format="gold" />.
- Officers can invite and buy skills, veterans invite and take items from the vault, members deposit and put items in.
- The guild grows on its own while you play: earn guild EXP and complete the three weekly goals to get the Rally.
- Wisdom or Expansion first, the rest later.
</Callout>

## Founding

Go to <Npc id="guildmaster" />. Pick a name and a short tag: the tag shows above every member's head, so keep it readable. The guild belongs to the character who founded it.

## Ranks

Only make people you trust officers: an officer can remove members, withdraw gold and spend the treasury on skills. The veteran rank is a good reward for regulars: they can invite and take a few items a day from the vault. The leader can change all this in the Settings tab.

## Level and weekly goals

Guild EXP comes on its own from play: monsters, Elites, bosses, quests, contracts, events, gathering, crafting and upgrading. The daily cap per character means ten players who play every day give the guild more than one who plays all day long. Grey monsters do not count.

At the start of the week look at the Goals tab. The three goals are one of fight, one of adventure and one of craft, so everyone can chip in. All three bring the Rally: more experience and gold for everyone for the whole next week. Whoever earned enough guild EXP in the week also collects Dragon Shards for each goal.

## Vault

Anyone can deposit, and the member list shows who gave how much. Agree on a simple rule, for example a share of the gold from every day of hunting. Leave gear for younger characters and crafting materials in the item vault; the log shows who took what.

## Which skills first

Further skill levels open with the guild level, so at first you can only buy the first ones anyway.


- **Wisdom** adds <Stat path="mechanics.guild.skills[wisdom].effects.0" format="pct" /> EXP per skill level for everyone. It pays off at once because it works for all members.
- **Expansion** adds <Stat path="mechanics.guild.skills[expansion].effects.0" /> places per level. Buy it when you run out of places for new players.
- **Luck** and **Fortune** add item chance and gold. They are expensive, so they are a later goal.
- **Healer's Grace** lowers potion prices. It is the cheapest, good for a start while the treasury is small.

## PvP protection

Guild members cannot attack each other. If you want to practise fighting each other, the leader can turn the protection off, but duels on the arena are the better way.
