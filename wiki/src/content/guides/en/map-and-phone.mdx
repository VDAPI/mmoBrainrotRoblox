<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- The world map shows only symbols. The plate's shape tells you what a thing is, and its colour which category it belongs to.
- Filters hide the categories you do not need; the game keeps your choice on the account.
- On a phone start with low graphics quality and the ☰ menu button, which holds every window.
</Callout>

## Symbols on the map

The world map has no fixed labels piling on top of each other. Every place and person is a symbol on a dark plate ringed in the category's colour, always the same size on screen at any zoom. Round plates are people, diamonds are ways, and squares are danger.

<MapLegend />

The name shows in the tooltip when you hover or tap, and on the card that opens on a click. Hunting grounds show only their levels. The "Labels" switch shows names wherever they cover nothing.

## Clusters

When several symbols sit in one place, the map spreads them apart. If there are more, they merge into a badge with a number. Click it and the map zooms in until they separate; at the closest zoom everything is spread out.

## Filters and search

The "Filters" panel has a chip for every category with its symbol and count. A click hides or shows the category, and "All" and "None" do it for all at once. The panel folds away; on a phone it starts folded. Your choice is saved on the account and also applies to the minimap, except the monster dots, which always show.

The search box above the map finds whatever is on the map; results carry the symbol of their category. The minimap uses the same symbols. When it is crowded it shows only the most important one, as a small plate has no room for a badge with a number.

## Playing on a phone

On the first entry from a phone the game sets low graphics quality by itself, and medium on a tablet. After that it is your call in the game menu.

On a phone held sideways every window sits in the drawer of the ☰ menu button next to the minimap. The button gets a "!" when something new waits inside, such as unspent points or an achievement. The attack button is in the bottom right corner, with the skills on an arc around it. The envelope button by the minimap opens the mail.

## Settings for weaker hardware

You find these options in the game menu:

- **Graphics quality: Low** cuts the most: fewer trees and bushes in the background, less decoration, only a few of the nearest lights shine, no shadows, glow or depth of field, and a third of the particles. Gameplay does not change; monsters and obstacles are the same.
- **World detail** sets the small ornaments in town and on the maps. With low graphics quality it is at most low.
- **Hit feel** turns off the screen shake and the short freeze on heavy blows if they tire you.
- **Low health warning** turns the red vignette and the heartbeat on and off.
- **Rotating minimap** turns the minimap with the camera or keeps north up.
