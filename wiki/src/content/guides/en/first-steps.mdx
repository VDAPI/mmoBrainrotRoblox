<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- Follow the main quests: they lead you through the city and the first region in the right order.
- Spend your points right after every level up, mostly on your class's main attribute.
- Death takes nothing from you, so do not be afraid to try.
</Callout>

## Controls

You move with WASD and turn the camera with the right mouse button. Game windows open with shortcuts, and your skills sit on the number keys. On a phone everything is on the on-screen buttons.

<KeybindTable />

Drink potions with <Key action="potion_hp" />, <Key action="potion_mana" /> and <Key action="potion_energy" />. Pick up loot with <Key action="pickup" /> and talk to characters with <Key action="interact" />.

## The first hour

You start on the market square. <Npc id="captain" /> gives you the first quest, and from then on the main story leads you by itself: a visit to the healer, to the blacksmith, out through the walls and your first hunt. A mark above a character shows who has a quest for you, and an arrow on screen leads to your goal. The quest journal opens with <Key action="win_quests" />.

## Where to level

This path shows the area the game recommends for every level, with the main quests of that range. Fight monsters close to your level: the colour of the level above a monster tells you whether it is a good target.

<LevelPath from={1} to={10} />

## Points after a level up

Every level up gives <Stat path="progression.statPointsPerLevel" /> attribute points. Warriors put them into Strength, Hunters into Dexterity, Mages and Clerics into Intellect, and some Vitality helps everyone. From level <Stat path="progression.skillPointsFromLevel" /> you also get a skill point: spend it in the <Key action="win_skills" /> window. You can plan your build first in the <Link to="classes">class planner</Link>.

## Zones

The zone colour tells you whether other players can attack you.

## When you die

You lose nothing. After a short timer you choose to stand up in the city or where you died, and for a moment after respawning nothing can hurt you. Details in <Link to="mechanics" id="death">Death</Link>.
