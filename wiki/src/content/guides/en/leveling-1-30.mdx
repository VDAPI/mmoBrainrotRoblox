<Callout kind="tip" title="In short">
- Fight monsters in your level colour: much weaker ones give less and less, down to almost nothing.
- Do main quests and daily contracts on the way, not instead of hunting.
- Elites, caves and playing in a party give more EXP per minute once you are ready.
</Callout>

## The level path

The game recommends an area for your level in the portal window and on the map. This is that path from the start to level thirty. Areas with a badge have their own character: some give faster EXP, others better loot or more gold.

<Callout kind="changed" date="2026-10-07" update="2026-10-07-expowiska">
Hunting grounds now have profiles: fast EXP, better loot or more gold at the same level. You see the profile next to the area in the portal window and on the map.
</Callout>

## Level gap

A monster more than <Stat path="progression.levelDiffRules.freeDiff" /> levels below you loses <Stat path="progression.levelDiffRules.penaltyPerLevel" format="frac" /> of its reward for every further level, down to <Stat path="progression.levelDiffRules.minMultiplier" format="frac" />. A stronger one gives a bonus, at most <Stat path="progression.levelDiffRules.maxBonus" format="frac" />. When the monsters of an area turn grey or green, it is time to move on.

## Elites and caves

An Elite gives <Stat path="mechanics.variants.elite.expMul" format="x" /> and an Elite II <Stat path="mechanics.variants.elite2.expMul" format="x" /> of the normal reward. Caves are full of elites, but they are a red zone: other players can attack you there. Go in with spare potions and preferably with a party.

## Quests and contracts

- Main quests give big chunks of EXP and lead to the next regions.
- Side quests of the Meadows folk end with a choice of an item for your class.
- The <Npc id="questboard" /> gives <Stat path="quests.daily.perDay" /> contracts a day. Their goals fit your level, so you finish them while hunting anyway.

## Parties and bonuses

In a party EXP is split between the members, but you kill much faster and more safely, especially elites. A Fortune line blessing and guild bonuses add a percentage to every EXP you gain. Details in <Link to="mechanics" id="experience">Experience</Link>.

## The next region

When the recommended area moves to a new region, the main story usually already leads there. First, though, swap your weapon and armour for the next tier: the <Link to="items">item database</Link> shows what to wear at your level.
