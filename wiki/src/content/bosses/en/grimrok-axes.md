---
boss: grimrok
phase: 1
title: Axes and fissures
---
The ground blow splits the earth along a lane in front of Grimrok: the crack runs from the boss, so step sideways off the line. Whoever keeps far away gets a fan of three axes that fly back to the chief's hand: dodge them twice. The landing after his leap sends out a low shock ring to jump over or wait out far away.
