---
boss: vaelgrath
phase: 3
title: Freezing storm
---
In phase 3, every {STORM_TICK} s the storm freezes everyone farther than {WARM_RADIUS} studs from a burning brazier. Stay close to the braziers in the corners of the arena. Since bosses 2.0 the dragon also weaves Absolute Zero in this phase: it stands still and you have to deal it enough damage before it finishes, or the frost hits the whole party. Go back to the braziers only after the spell is broken.
