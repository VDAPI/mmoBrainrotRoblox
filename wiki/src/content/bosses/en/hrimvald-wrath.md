---
boss: hrimvald
phase: 1
title: Wrath of the giant
---
Most of Hrimvald's blows are things you run from. A boulder flies in an arc at one player. Ice spikes run along the ground in three lanes. Ice blocks fall on several players at once. The stomp sends out a low ring you have to jump over. The shard rain drops small icicles over the whole field. The frost breath turns as a cone in front of the giant. A javelin volley flies in a fan at one player, then at another.
