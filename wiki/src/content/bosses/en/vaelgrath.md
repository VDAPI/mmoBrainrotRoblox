---
boss: vaelgrath
phase: 2
title: Flight over the arena
---
At the start of phase 2 the dragon takes to the air for {FLIGHT_SECONDS} s above a wave of dragonspawn that shields it. It cannot be hit until it has landed and the whole wave is down: clear the wave first, then turn back to the dragon. After the landing the Frost Tomb comes back: the marked player walks away from the party before the ice bursts around them.
